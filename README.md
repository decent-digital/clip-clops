# Clip Clops

This was an experimental atproto feed displaying all video posts as they came in on bluesky. I made a day or two after video became available on bluesky, both to explore the feature and learn how feeds function on the platform (and larger atproto ecosystem).

It was disabled about 24 hours later due to issues with filtering. Lots of very unsavory videos appeared right away, and the feed spread widely enough that people were telling me my (free! experimental!) feed was unsafe. A short lived experiment. Leaving it up as an archive for reference.
